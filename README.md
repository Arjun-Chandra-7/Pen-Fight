## 🕹️ Controls & Gameplay Modes

1. **Career League:** Challenge 5 scheduled rivals (Kavi, Nisha, Omar, Mira, Ash) to win their pens and fill your case.
2. **Practice Duel:** Safe training grounds to test pen contact points and angles without stakes.
3. **Marathon 50:** Race to 50 wins against an adaptive AI bot that learns your shot power, bias, and angles in real-time.
