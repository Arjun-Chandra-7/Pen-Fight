# Last Bench League - Pen Fight Game

An original browser pen-battle game with a retro classroom hub, customizable pencil case, realistic desk physics, adaptive AI rival, and Google Authentication.

## 🚀 Live Game & Deployment
- **Live Vercel Link:** [https://pen-fight-lemon.vercel.app](https://pen-fight-lemon.vercel.app)
- **GitHub Repository:** [https://github.com/Arjun-Chandra-7/Pen-Fight](https://github.com/Arjun-Chandra-7/Pen-Fight)

---

## 🔑 Features

### Google Authentication
- Integrated with **Google Identity Services (GSI)** for official Google Sign-In.
- Cloud save syncing and local storage session persistence.
- Verified developer authentication with private access control.

### 🕹️ Controls & Gameplay Modes
1. **Career League:** Challenge 5 scheduled rivals (Kavi, Nisha, Omar, Mira, Ash) to win their pens and fill your case.
2. **Practice Duel:** Safe training grounds to test pen contact points and angles without stakes.
3. **Marathon 50:** Race to 50 wins against an adaptive AI bot that learns your shot power, bias, and angles in real-time.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build steps or heavy dependencies required.
